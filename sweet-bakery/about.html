Our Story

Sweet Bakery started as a small family kitchen and grew into a neighbourhood favourite. Every loaf and pastry is still made fresh each morning using recipes passed down through the family, with a focus on quality ingredients and honest baking.

What We Believe In

We believe good bread brings people together. Whether you're grabbing a quick breakfast pastry or ordering a cake for a once-in-a-lifetime celebration, we want every visit to feel personal and every bite to feel like home.
